Ambition for Korea, a one day hackathon for Korea's AI builders who are serious about going global

Hosted together by Anthropic, the Seoul Metropolitan Government, Seoul AI Hub, six venture capital firms, and the Bloom community.

150 people gather at Seoul AI Hub at 9 AM. You take part as a team of 2 to 4, but applications and approvals are handled one person at a time. Even if you come as a team, every member has to apply on this page individually. You can apply without a team, and after approval you will find teammates in a dedicated channel on the Bloom Discord. The mission is revealed when the event starts, and it will look something like a service for knowledge worker freelancers in the US.

Every participant receives $500 worth of Claude credits. Across all 150 builders that is $75,000 worth of credits released in a single day. Everyone works with the same resources, and the single criterion is how much value you create with them.

⚡ How it works

Submission is first come first served.

On the day, the submission window is open until 5 PM. You submit a demo link and a deck of no more than two slides. VCs screen each submission in the order it arrives, and the queue closes the moment 8 teams have passed. Submitting early will not help if the work is not ready, and being ready will not help if 8 teams are already through. The remaining slots are shown on screen in real time, so everyone builds while watching how many are left.

Each team gets one submission. What you submit is what gets judged, and you cannot submit again.

If you do not pass, or if the 8 slots are already taken, your run in the competition ends there. You are still welcome to stay and watch the evening demo day.

The evening is a shark tank.

The 8 finalist teams present to the six VC firms, who judge together in the room. Not every team gets matched, and the teams that do move on to a one on one meeting for actual investment review.
